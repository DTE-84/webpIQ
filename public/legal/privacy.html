Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Overview

WebPIQ (the "App") is an image conversion tool operated by DTE Solutions LLC ("we," "our," or "us"). This policy explains what information the App handles, which is very little by design. It applies to the WebPIQ website, the installable web app, and the WebPIQ mobile apps.

2. Your photos stay on your device

Every image you add to WebPIQ is processed locally, in your browser or on your phone. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers or to any third party, and we cannot see them.

Conversion, resizing, padding, cropping, renaming, metadata removal, and ZIP creation all happen on your device. When you close or clear the App, the images held in memory are discarded. Files you save or share are written only where you choose.

3. Information we collect

  • No account. You do not sign up, and we do not ask for your name, email, or contact details to use the App.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking. The App does not currently include analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or third-party trackers, and does not use cookies. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change takes effect.
  • Purchase information. If you buy WebPIQ Pro, the payment is handled by our payment processor or the app store you buy from (see Section 5). We receive the details needed to fulfil and support the order, such as an order reference and the email address you provided at checkout. We do not receive your full card number.
  • Hosting logs. Like any website, the host that serves the web app may automatically log technical data such as IP address and browser type under its own policies. We do not use this to identify you.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send, and we use it only to respond.

4. Information stored on your device

The App stores a small amount of data locally on your device, and it does not leave your device:

  • Your Pro license key, if you activate one.
  • Your saved presets (sizes and settings you chose to save).
  • Cached copies of the App's own files so it can work offline.

You can remove this by clearing the site's data in your browser, removing your license in the App, or uninstalling the app.

5. Third parties

  • Stripe processes payments for purchases made on our website. Stripe's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play process purchases and distribution for the mobile apps and are governed by their own policies.
  • Web hosting is provided by a third-party static hosting service that delivers the App's files to your browser.
  • Open-source components (such as image encoding and ZIP libraries) are bundled into the App and run locally. They do not send your images or data anywhere.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

6. Photo and file access (mobile apps)

The mobile apps access only the photos and files you explicitly select, using your device's standard picker. The App does not scan your library in the background. Files you convert are shared or saved through your device's share sheet, to the destination you choose.

7. Image metadata

Exported images are re-encoded, which removes embedded metadata such as EXIF data, camera details, and GPS location. We recommend you still review sensitive images before publishing them. Metadata in your original files is not changed.

8. Data retention

Because we do not receive or store your images, there are no images for us to retain or delete. Purchase and support records are kept only as long as needed to provide the service, handle refunds and disputes, and meet tax, accounting, and legal obligations.

9. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, email support@dte-solutions.icu. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Because your images are processed on your own device and not transmitted to us, they are not exposed to us or to our servers. The App is delivered over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest revision. For material changes we will provide notice in the App or on our website.
